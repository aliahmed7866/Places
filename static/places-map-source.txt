Build: tools/build_atlas.py with shapely; simplify .025 degrees preserving topology.
Projection: equirectangular. Countries and territories are counted together.
Focus/label positions use the largest land component to avoid overseas-area jumps.
Boundaries follow the source's de facto treatment and are not a sovereignty claim.
Small territories absent from the geometry remain available in country search.
